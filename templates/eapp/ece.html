ELECTRONICS AND COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) was established in 1980 and has since met the growing demands of industry and society. The department has seven Ph.D. holders, and many faculty members are pursuing their Ph.D. in various areas of ECE. The staff members are well-qualified and experienced. The department has state-of-the-art laboratory facilities. It has an undergraduate intake of 240 students.
