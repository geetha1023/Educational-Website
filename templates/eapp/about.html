About Us:

NBKRIST Commitment:

The Vision, Mission, Core Values, and Quality Policy of NBKR Institute of Science & Technology together form the foundation of the institution’s academic excellence, ethical leadership, innovation, and social contribution. These principles guide every initiative undertaken in the pursuit of future-ready engineers and responsible citizens.
Promote high-quality research, innovation, and publications.
Maintain global standards of excellence in teaching, consultancy, and creativity.
Collaborate with industries and institutes for skill development, knowledge sharing, and entrepreneurship.

Vision:

To emerge as a comprehensive institute that provides quality technical education and research, thereby building a valuable human resource for industry and society. Long-term academic aspiration of the institution.NBKRIST is committed to shaping future-ready engineers through disciplined learning, research orientation, innovation, and values-driven education. These principles guide every academic and administrative initiative undertaken by the institution.

Mission:

1.Learner-Centered Environment
Provide an environment that encourages active participation and continuous engagement in the educational process.
2.Faculty Empowerment
Empower faculty to excel in teaching while engaging in research, creativity, and public service.
3.Professional and Ethical Growth
Develop effective learning skills, critical thinking, and professional competence with strong ethical values.
4.Industry and Community Connection
Reach out to industries, schools, and public agencies to partner and share human and academic resources.

Core Values:

1.Integrity
Adhering to the highest standards of honesty, fairness, trust, and integrity in personal and professional behavior.
2.Excellence
Persistent commitment to hard work, diligence, perseverance, and consistency in the pursuit of the highest quality.
3.Social Responsibility
Providing opportunities that enhance lives and enable intellectual, economic, social, and cultural growth.
4.Continuous Improvement
Imbibing a culture of steady, meaningful, and measurable improvement across all institutional activities.
5.Equitability
Appreciating diversity in the student body, faculty, and staff through civility and a commitment to tolerance.
